# The Process

*The generative method. How the Nothingness Generator runs.*

---

This section describes the **act of generating**, not the things produced. The standing structures the generator yields — Closure, Polarity, Self-Reference, and so on — live in [`03-the-grammar/`](../03-the-grammar/). What lives here is the method itself: the dialectical unfolding by which the postulates produce structure, and the discipline required to apply it honestly.

## What the process is

The process is a method of **paraconsistent dialectical unfolding** from minimal postulates, performed empathically from within, and structurally self-bootstrapping. It has five operating principles plus one meta-property:

### Operating principles

1. **Hold the postulates in productive tension.** [Conservation](../01-postulates/conservation.md), [Existence](../01-postulates/existence.md), and [Internality](../01-postulates/internality.md) appear contradictory under classical logic. The process treats this tension as the engine, not the failure. (See [logical-foundation.md](../00-orientation/logical-foundation.md) for the lineage.)

2. **At each step, ask what the previous step requires.** Do not import outside content. Do not reach for familiar structures. Track only what the prior commitments demand under the framework's logic.

3. **Distinguish forced moves from chosen moves.** A forced move is one no alternative satisfies. A chosen move is one the generator could resolve differently and resolves *this* way for stated reasons. Both are admissible; conflating them is not.

4. **Identify atoms at every level.** Working from first principles requires that, at each level of articulation, the irreducible elements be named explicitly. Anything non-atomic is composite of atoms; the chain from any structure back to its atoms must in principle be traceable.

5. **Run the generator from within.** By Internality and Self-Reference, the runner of the generator is internal to what is being articulated. Running requires *empathic entrance*: putting oneself into the structural position being investigated and tracking what unfolds from within. This is not decorative; it is the only access available.

### Meta-property

**The generator self-bootstraps.** Applied to the question of its own structure, the generator yields its own structure: the three postulates emerge as atoms of commitment, the four grammar items as atoms of standing structure, and the five operating principles as atoms of methodology. This is forced by Internality + Self-Reference: a generator that did not self-bootstrap would have external origins, violating its own postulates. (See [self-bootstrap.md](self-bootstrap.md).)

## What this section does *not* contain

- Specific algebraic structures the generator produces (see [`03-the-grammar/`](../03-the-grammar/)).
- Specific applications to physics, language, biology, or any other domain (see [`10-experiments/`](../10-experiments/)).
