# The Dialectical Unfolding

*How the generator generates.*

---

## The starting tension

The three postulates, held simultaneously, are in productive tension:

- **Conservation** says the totality sums to nothing.
- **Existence** says something is.
- **Internality** says nothing is outside.

Under classical logic, *Conservation ∧ Existence* looks like a contradiction. Under paraconsistent dialectical logic, this tension is the engine. It cannot be resolved by removing a postulate — each removal collapses the framework. It can only be *unfolded*: held, articulated, and let to produce structure.

This is the generator's first move and its constant operation: **hold the tension, and ask what it requires**.

"Holding" is not a metaphor. It is an empathic act — sustained attention to the tension from within, without resolution, until structural requirements become apparent. See [the empathic mode](the-empathic-mode.md).

## The discipline

At each level of articulation, the process performs the same act:

1. **Recognize the tension** at the current level.
2. **Ask what holding it requires.** What further structure must be present for the tension to remain held without collapse?
3. **Articulate that structure** explicitly.
4. **Repeat.** The new structure introduces its own tensions, requiring further articulation.

The generator does not invent structure. It tracks what is *demanded* by the prior tensions when they are held under the framework's logic.

## The first turning of the wheel

Applied to the founding postulates:

- Conservation requires the totality balance to nothing.
- Existence requires something be.
- For both to hold, what *is* must balance against what *is*. → [Polarity (weak form)](../03-the-grammar/02-polarity.md).

Then:

- Internality forbids external bookkeeping.
- The compensation must be sustained from within.
- The sustaining act is itself part of the totality. → [Self-Reference](../03-the-grammar/03-self-reference.md).

These are what the generator strictly forces. Beyond them, every further turning requires *additional commitments* (about minimality, specific algebraic forms, what counts as "stable"). Those are not in the postulates and must be added explicitly.

## What the unfolding *is*, viewed cleanly

The unfolding is not a list of theorems. It is a **discipline of attention** applied to the postulates. The discipline asks, at every level: *what does holding this tension require?* The answer, when traced honestly, produces structure.

Every legitimate piece of content in this repo is retraceable to this discipline applied to the postulates plus whatever further commitments have been explicitly named. Anything that cannot be retraced is decoration.

## How the unfolding fails

The unfolding produces unsupported content when:

- **Importing structure.** Reaching for a specific algebraic form, topology, or notion of "minimum" when prior steps do not demand it.
- **Conflating commitment with derivation.** Claiming a structure is *forced* when it is *chosen* under an additional principle.
- **Resolving the tension.** Treating the founding paradox as something to be eliminated. Collapses the generator and stops the unfolding.
- **Skipping levels.** Asserting downstream structure without showing the chain of requirements that produced it.

The discipline of the process is the discipline of avoiding these failures.

## The role of paraconsistent logic

Classical logic forbids contradiction. The generator's first move *is* a contradiction. If the generator obeyed classical logic, it could not start.

Paraconsistent logic permits contradiction without explosion. Dialectical logic adds that contradiction is *productive* — holding $P \wedge \neg P$ generates structure that subsumes both. Hegel called this *Aufhebung*.

The combination — paraconsistent + dialectical — is the logical engine of the process. See [logical-foundation.md](../00-orientation/logical-foundation.md) for the broader lineage.
