# Glossary

*Quick reference for the framework's vocabulary.*

---

## Core terms

**Totality** — Everything that exists, taken as one. The thing the framework articulates. By the postulates, the totality is closed (no outside), balanced ($\Sigma = 0$), self-observing, and self-relating.

**The Nothingness Generator** — The framework as a whole, defined by its generative process rather than by the structures it produces. The "Nothingness" refers to $\Sigma = 0$ as the starting condition; the "Generator" refers to the dialectical method that unfolds structure from that condition.

**Generator** — The methodological core: the dialectical-empathic process applied to the postulates. Domain-neutral. Runs on whatever the postulates plus chosen commitments produce.

**Articulation** — The act of unfolding structure from the postulates and commitments. The generator's primary activity.

## The three postulates

**Conservation** — $\Sigma = 0$. The totality sums to zero. The strongest non-vacuous symmetry constraint.

**Existence** — $\exists$. Something is, and is observed. The minimum commitment that defeats vacuity. Read in the strong sense: existence is constitutively observed.

**Internality** — No outside. Distinctions are sustained internally. Forbids external bookkeeping, observers, or causes. The framework's commitment to *radical immanence*.

## The four standing properties (the grammar)

**Closure** — The totality has no exterior. Direct restatement of Internality.

**Polarity (weak form)** — Non-zero contribution requires compensating contribution somewhere in the totality. From Conservation + Existence.

**Self-Reference** — Every internal observation references something internal; complete description includes itself. From Internality + Existence-as-observation.

**Internality of Relations** — Relations between elements of the totality are themselves internal to the totality. Direct corollary of Internality applied to relational structure.

## The five operating principles (the process)

**Productive tension** — Holding the apparent contradiction between Conservation and Existence as the engine of unfolding, not as a contradiction to resolve.

**Requirement tracking** — At each step, asking only what the prior commitments demand. Never importing outside content.

**Forced vs. chosen** — Every move is labeled: either no alternative satisfies (forced), or alternatives exist and one is selected with stated reasons (chosen).

**Atomicity at every level** — Identifying what is irreducible at each scale of articulation. Anything non-atomic is composite of atoms. The "seeking of atoms" is required by first-principles thinking; the "flavor of atomicity" (algebraic, topological, etc.) is chosen.

**Empathic mode** — Running the generator from within. By Internality, the runner is internal to what is being articulated; running is sustained attention to the structural position from inside, not external manipulation.

## Meta-property

**Self-bootstrap** — The generator is articulable as the generator running on the question of its own structure. Forced by Internality + Self-Reference. Demonstrates internal coherence and self-grounding; not classical self-proof.

## Levels of rigor

**Theorem** — A formal proof from prior content (e.g., consequences of standard algebra applied to a chosen equation).

**Construction** — A step that follows from prior content under the framework's paraconsistent dialectical logic. Not a Hilbert-style proof.

**Observation** — A structural claim defended by reasoning but not formally derived.

**Commitment** — A chosen move beyond the postulates, with stated reasons. An experiment is a chain of commitments plus consequences.

**Assignment** — A specific identification within an experiment (this domain element corresponds to that framework structure). A revisable hypothesis.

## Methodological terms

**Postulate** — An irreducible starting commitment. Cannot be derived. The framework has three.

**Grammar** — The standing properties of the totality strictly forced by the postulates. The framework has four.

**Process** — The generative method itself: the dialectical unfolding plus its operating principles.

**Experiment** — The generator articulating itself through a specific lens (a slice of the totality). Adds additional commitments beyond the postulates. Lives in [`10-experiments/`](../10-experiments/). Note: the conventional phrasing "applying the generator to a domain" is retained for readability, but the deeper grammar is reflexive — an experiment is the totality articulating itself in a specific form, not external manipulation.

**Lens** — A particular slice of the totality through which the generator can articulate its structure. Synonymous with "domain" in this framework's usage; "lens" emphasizes the reflexive grammar (the generator articulating *through* the lens, not *to* a domain).

**Commitment chain** — The sequence of additional commitments an experiment makes beyond the postulates. Each chain produces a specific articulation in a specific domain.

**Atom** — Whatever is irreducible at a given level of articulation, given the prior commitments at that level.

**Boundary** — The line between what the postulates strictly force (the grammar) and what requires additional commitments. Documented at [`03-the-grammar/05-boundary.md`](../03-the-grammar/05-boundary.md).

## Logical terms

**Paraconsistent logic** — Logic where contradictions do not entail explosion. Formal works: Newton da Costa, Graham Priest.

**Dialetheism** — The view that some contradictions are *true*. Graham Priest.

**Generative dialectics** — Hegelian: contradiction held until new structure emerges that subsumes both terms. *Aufhebung* (sublation).

**Productive tension** — A held contradiction that produces structure rather than collapsing the system. The generator's engine.

## Outcomes for an experiment

**Hit** — Predictions match the domain.

**Miss** — Predictions don't match. Either an assignment is wrong, a commitment is wrong, or the framework doesn't speak to this phenomenon.

**Silence** — The framework has nothing structural to say. Maps the boundary of the experiment's commitments.

**Success with strain** — Predictions match but assignments needed adjustment. Informs how the experiment should be developed.

## Lineage shorthand

**Pythagorean–Platonic tradition** — The genre this framework belongs to: structural metaphysics in which mathematical pattern and ontological structure are aspects of one thing.

**Coincidentia oppositorum** — Cusa: opposites coincide at the infinite. The framework's founding tension is one instance.

**Strange loop** — Hofstadter: self-reference as the substrate of mind. The framework's Self-Reference is structurally similar.

**Prehension** — Whitehead: each occasion takes account of its predecessors by entering them. Cognate with the empathic mode.

**Catuṣkoṭi** — Nāgārjuna's fourfold logic: (1) true, (2) false, (3) both, (4) neither. The classical example of a non-classical logic that handles paradox without explosion.
