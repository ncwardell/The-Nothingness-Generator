# Honest Audit

*What is cleanly factual, what is structural elegance, what is interpretive overlay — at the generator level.*

---

This document audits the **generator** itself. Specific experiments perform their own audits within their own folders, where domain-specific facts can be assessed against domain-specific commitments. The audit here covers only what the framework claims about itself, independent of any experiment.

## A. Cleanly factual content of the generator

These survive any audit, independent of any interpretation of the framework:

- **The three postulates.** Conservation, Existence, Internality. Stated as postulates, defended as a minimal jointly-generative starting point. Not provable; not intended to be.
- **Closure as a restatement of Internality.** A direct vocabulary mapping; not a substantive theorem.
- **Polarity (weak form) as a consequence of Conservation + Existence.** Strict arithmetic on sums. Rigorous given the standard reading of "sum."
- **Self-Reference as a consequence of Internality + Existence-as-observation.** Rigorous given the strong reading of Existence (i.e., that existence is constitutively observed). The reading is itself a commitment of the framework, made explicit in [`01-postulates/existence.md`](../01-postulates/existence.md).
- **Internality of Relations as a corollary of Internality.** Direct application of "no outside" to the relational structure of the totality, not just its elements.
- **The boundary marker.** The explicit acknowledgment that nothing richer than the four standing properties is forced *at the framework level* by the postulates. Anything past that point is committed, not derived — at the framework level. (Within a lens, the lens's commitments produce their own forced cascades; see [framework-level vs lens-level forcing](../03-the-grammar/05-boundary.md#framework-level-vs-lens-level-forcing).)

That is the entirety of the generator's strict framework-level content. It is small. The smallness is the point.

## B. Methodological practices of the generator

These are practices the framework follows in how it operates. Several are *structurally forced* by the postulates' implications — the underlying structure is forced even when the practice of following the discipline is a methodological choice. Each item below notes whether its underlying structure is **forced** or **chosen**.

- **Forced-vs-chosen discipline.** Every move in any unfolding must be labeled. *Structure: chosen* — without this discipline, the framework still operates; with it, the framework is auditable. (See [`02-the-process/forced-vs-chosen.md`](../02-the-process/forced-vs-chosen.md).)
- **Atomicity at every level.** *Structure: forced by Internality + Self-Reference.* Atoms must exist at every level of articulation — there is no external grounding for composites; witnessing requires minimal articulable forms. The *practice* of explicitly naming atoms at each level is a methodological discipline; the *structural requirement* that atoms exist is forced by the postulates. (See [`02-the-process/atoms.md`](../02-the-process/atoms.md).)
- **Empathic running.** *Structure: forced by Self-Reference + Internality.* The runner is internal to the totality; running from outside is structurally impossible because there is no outside, and witnessing what prevents polarity collapse requires the runner being internal. The *practice* of running deliberately from inside is the methodological form. (See [`02-the-process/the-empathic-mode.md`](../02-the-process/the-empathic-mode.md).)
- **Self-bootstrap.** *Structure: forced by Internality + Self-Reference.* It demonstrates internal coherence and self-grounding; it is not classical self-proof. (See [`02-the-process/self-bootstrap.md`](../02-the-process/self-bootstrap.md).)
- **Paraconsistent dialectical logic.** *Structure: forced* — the postulates contain productive tension ($\Sigma = 0 \wedge \exists$) that classical logic would render explosive. The *choice* of which paraconsistent system (da Costa, Priest, etc.) is methodological. (See [`00-orientation/logical-foundation.md`](../00-orientation/logical-foundation.md).)
- **Internality of articulation.** *Structure: forced by Internality.* No experiment may invoke external bookkeeping or external standards. All structure is articulated from within the totality.
- **Experiment structure.** *Structure: chosen* in specific format, but the *requirement* of explicit commitments and consequences is forced by forced-vs-chosen + atomicity. Every experiment must state its phenomenon, its commitments, its assignments, its consequences, and its comparison to the domain.

The split: where structure is forced, the framework cannot operate without it (the postulates require it). Where structure is chosen, the framework could operate differently but has selected this discipline for defensibility and clarity. Following both — the forced structures faithfully and the chosen disciplines as committed — is what makes the framework defensible as a structural ontology rather than as ad hoc speculation.

## C. Lineage and genre

The framework belongs to the Pythagorean–Platonic tradition of structural metaphysics, articulated in modern technical register. The lineage is documented in [`README.md`](../README.md) and [`00-orientation/logical-foundation.md`](../00-orientation/logical-foundation.md).

Belonging to a lineage is not by itself evidence of correctness. It is evidence of *genre* — what kind of project this is, and what evaluative standards apply. The standards of structural metaphysics are *internal coherence*, *cross-domain productivity*, and *generative depth*. The framework is properly evaluated against those standards, not against the standards of empirical physics or classical mathematics.

## D. What is *not* at the generator level

Things often associated with this kind of framework, but absent at the framework level — they live in lens-level experiments where they belong:

- **No specific algebraic structures.** No equations, no specific constants, no specific eigenvalues. These live in the [math experiment](../10-experiments/mathematics/) (algebra subdomain).
- **No specific topology.** No tori, no manifolds, no graphs, no specific spaces. These live in the [topology experiment](../10-experiments/topology/) (skeleton).
- **No specific dynamics.** No specific flow, no specific recurrence, no specific time evolution. These live in the [dynamics experiment](../10-experiments/dynamics/) (skeleton) and downstream lenses.
- **No specific vocabulary for structural positions.** No names for specific elements of an unfolding. These live in experiments (in particular, in the [language experiment](../10-experiments/language/)).
- **No quantitative predictions.** The generator does not predict numbers. Experiments may, given their commitments.
- **No claims about specific domains.** The generator is domain-neutral. Domain claims live in experiments.

If you find any of the above in core documents (`00-orientation/`, `01-postulates/`, `02-the-process/`, `03-the-grammar/`), they have leaked from an experiment and should be removed or relabeled. Lens-level forced consequences belong in their lens, not at the framework level.

## E. What would change the evaluation of the generator

The generator's status would shift in either direction based on:

**Toward stronger** —

- Demonstrations that the three postulates are jointly sufficient for richer strict consequences at the framework level than currently identified, expanding the strict framework-level grammar (currently the four standing properties).
- Articulation of new lens-level forced cascades in additional experiments (dynamics, topology, biology, etc.), each closing on its own bootstrap and contributing to cross-lens transferability.
- Multiple successful experiments using related but not identical commitments, showing that the generator's method is robust across different commitment chains.
- A novel experiment in a previously-untouched domain that produces non-trivial structural insights with minimal commitment.
- Cross-lens convergences (the same forced atom appearing in genuinely different lenses with different commitment chains) — the strongest form of evidence that a structure is generator-level rather than lens-specific.

**Toward weaker** —

- Demonstration that the postulates are inconsistent in a way that paraconsistent logic does not rescue.
- Demonstration that the framework's experiments share *no* method other than vocabulary — i.e., the generator does no work; the experiments are independent ad hoc constructions.
- Persistent failure across diverse experiments to produce non-trivial constructions, suggesting the generator's method is empty.

Both directions remain open. The work in [`10-experiments/`](../10-experiments/) is what moves the evaluation, and each experiment carries its own per-experiment audit.

## Bottom line

The generator's strict content is small and clean. Its methodological commitments are explicit. Its lineage is well-attested. Its productive power is to be evaluated experiment by experiment, with [`15-cross-domain-analysis/`](../15-cross-domain-analysis/) as the place where what we learn across experiments accumulates and where candidate refinements to the framework are tracked.

Honest articulation foregrounds (A) and (B) as the case for taking the framework seriously, and treats (D) as a discipline that prevents leakage between levels. Experiments earn or fail their own keep on their own terms; the generator persists either way. Cross-domain analysis is where the framework grows or proves limited, with explicit tracking of what changes and why.
