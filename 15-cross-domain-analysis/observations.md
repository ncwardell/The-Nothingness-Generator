## Current observations

### The four-structure $\{\phi, \psi, 1, -1\}$

**Statement:** A four-element structure with two kinds of polar relationships — an additive polar pair $\{1, -1\}$ and a multiplicative polar pair $\{\phi, \psi\}$ (where $\phi\psi = -1$ and $\phi + \psi = 1$) — emerges as a forced atom from the framework's seed when articulated through any lens admitting both algebraic structure and structural polarity.

**Appears in:**
- **Math experiment (algebra subdomain)** — produced via Vieta's relations on the self-reference equation $x^2 = x + 1$. The two roots $\phi, \psi$ are eigenvalues of self-reference; their sum is $1$; their product is $-1$. See [`mathematics/algebra/construction/03-vietas-relations.md`](../10-experiments/mathematics/algebra/construction/03-vietas-relations.md).
- **Topology experiment (Phase III)** — produced via bisection of the 6-element double triangle. The 3 nodes of one chirality are assigned to $\{\phi, \psi, 1\}$; the polar matching gives $\{-\phi, -\psi, -1\}$; the four-structure is the named subset of 4 of these 6 elements. See [`topology/construction/07-the-bisection.md`](../10-experiments/topology/construction/07-the-bisection.md).

**Shared commitments across these experiments:**
- The three postulates (Conservation, Existence, Internality).
- The four standing properties (Closure, Polarity, Self-Reference, Internality of Relations).
- The framework's discipline (forced-vs-chosen, atomicity, paraconsistent dialectical logic).
- Use of the algebraic value system $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt 5)$ that contains $\phi, \psi$.

**Distinct commitments across these experiments:**
- **Math** commits to: binary polarity (C1), labels (C2), addition as binary form of $\Sigma$ (C3), iteration (C4, reframed as forced), Fibonacci recurrence (C5, reframed as forced), and algebraic articulation of dynamics (A1).
- **Topology** commits to: nodes/edges/directedness (T1) and connectivity / no internally-disjoint substructures (T2). Plus the algebraic assignment in Topic 07.
- These are non-overlapping lens-level commitments; the four-structure emerges via genuinely independent cascade chains.

**Counter-experiment attempted:**
None yet. The dynamics experiment is at Phase I (no four-structure work yet); language is bootstrap-verified but uses math's algebraic template directly (shared chain — does not constitute independent convergence). Future experiments (physics, biology) should test whether the four-structure also emerges in their lenses with non-shared commitment chains.

**Strength:** Strong (pending counter-experiment validation). Two genuinely independent paths converging on the same forced atom.

**Notes:**
- This is the framework's first substantive cross-domain finding. The convergence is not a numerical coincidence — both lenses produce the same 4 algebraic values *with the same polar relationships* (additive between $1, -1$; multiplicative between $\phi, \psi$).
- The convergence supports the framework's [strong hypothesis](the-strong-hypothesis.md) at the structural level: the four-structure is a forced atom of the *generator*, not lens-specific.
- Candidate refinement: could the four-structure be promoted from "forced consequence per lens" to "framework-level structural fact"? See [`candidate-refinements.md`](candidate-refinements.md).
- Test prediction: any subsequent lens that engages the framework's seed (Conservation + Existence + the witnessing requirement) should articulate the four-structure or its lens-specific analog. Physics is the next test.
