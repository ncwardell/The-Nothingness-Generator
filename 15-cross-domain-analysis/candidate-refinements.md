# Candidate Refinements

*Proposed updates to the framework, drawn from cross-domain analysis.*

---

This document holds proposals before they are either promoted into the framework or rejected. Each candidate is tracked openly so the framework's growth is visible and accountable.

## Why have this document

Without explicit candidate-tracking, frameworks grow by accretion — new properties get added because they "feel right" without explicit justification. With this document, every change has a paper trail: what motivated it, what evidence supported it, what alternatives were considered, what was decided and why.

This is the framework's growth log.

[Reasons against promotion. Must include:
