## What this module is

Vacuum is a subdomain of [physics](../physics.md). It articulates *what the empty totality is and what inhabits it before observation* — the quantum vacuum, its fluctuations, and the virtual particles of quantum field theory (QFT).

The overlay it performs: **QFT's virtual particle is the physics name for the framework's unwitnessed polar pair.** The correspondence is not invented here — the [dynamics](../../dynamics/dynamics.md) chain already names it in its comparison and transferability notes. This subdomain is the physics instantiation: it takes the domain-neutral dynamics result (unwitnessed polar pairs are undifferentiated under iteration) and articulates it in the vocabulary of fields, quanta, and measurement.

This file is the **module overview**. The atomic step-by-step articulation lives in [`construction/`](construction/).

## Why this overlay is worth taking seriously

Most of the physics experiment ([spectrum](../spectrum/spectrum.md), [forces](../forces/forces.md)) makes contact with reality through *numerical* agreement — coupling constants and mass ratios matched to measured values. Numerical hits are strong evidence but carry the standing risk of post-hoc curve-fitting, and the [honest audit](../../../30-appendix/audit.md) flags this.

The vacuum overlay is a different *kind* of contact, and arguably a cleaner one. It is **structural, not numerical**. It claims no constant. It maps one qualitative structure (the framework's polar pair plus its witnessing requirement) onto another (QFT's virtual pair plus its promotion to real quanta) and asks whether the *shape* transfers. Because there is no number to fit, the usual curve-fitting objection does not apply. The overlay either matches the structure of vacuum physics or it doesn't. Where it matches, the match is meaningful; where it fails, the failure is visible. Both are recorded honestly below and per topic.

## Forced vs. chosen

**Chosen:**

- The lens itself: reading the quantum vacuum through the framework.
- V1, V2, V3 — the three identifications (vacuum $\leftrightarrow$ undifferentiated sum; virtual pair $\leftrightarrow$ unwitnessed polar pair; promotion $\leftrightarrow$ acquiring a third). Each is a substantive interpretive commitment, not a derivation.
- The identification of "the conserved quantity" with *additive quantum numbers* (charge, lepton/baryon number) rather than with energy-momentum. This choice is what makes the overlay clean (see honest assessment).

**Forced (given the choices):**

- That virtual pairs carry net-zero additive quantum numbers — this is standard QFT and matches V2 exactly.
- That a lone real photon cannot produce a real pair in vacuum (a third body is required) — standard QFT, and the framework's [two-node instability](../../dynamics/construction/02-two-node-instability.md) forces the structural analog independently.
- The appear/annihilate oscillation as the negation orbit $A \to -A \to A$ with sole fixed point $0$ — forced by the imported dynamics, matching the qualitative QFT picture.

## Honest assessment — the three tiers

Following the [audit](../../../30-appendix/audit.md) discipline, the overlay's contents separate into three tiers of strength. The topic files carry the detail; the summary:

**Tier 1 — genuine structural matches (the overlay earns these):**

- **Vacuum as balanced, not empty.** QFT's vacuum is the ground state of the field, full of structure, not nothing. $\Sigma = 0$ read as "balanced totality, not empty set" is the same move. Clean.
- **Net-zero quantum numbers.** A virtual pair sums to zero in every additive quantum number. This is exactly the polar pair $\{E, -E\}$. Clean and non-trivial.
- **The third-body requirement.** $\gamma \to e^+e^-$ is forbidden in vacuum; a nucleus or second photon must absorb recoil. The framework forces "a non-complement third is required for a polar pair to become a distinction" *independently*, from [minimum-cycle](../../dynamics/construction/03-minimum-cycle-requirement.md). Two derivations, one conclusion, is the strongest form of evidence the framework recognizes. This is the overlay's best hit.
- **Boundaries make the vacuum measurable.** Casimir plates, a bound electron (Lamb shift), an external field (Schwinger, Hawking) — each is a structure imposed on the undifferentiated vacuum that renders its polar content measurable. "A witness makes the undifferentiated distinct" reads these cleanly.

**Tier 2 — interpretive stance (defensible, but a choice, not a fact):**

- **Ontologizing virtual particles.** The overlay treats virtual pairs as *genuinely present but undifferentiated*. Mainstream QFT frequently treats them as calculational artifacts — internal lines in a perturbation series, not entities. The framework sides with the "vacuum fluctuations are real" reading. That is a legitimate philosophical position, not a settled fact, and the overlay's Tier-1 matches do not depend on winning this debate.

**Tier 3 — where the overlay is loose or silent (stated plainly):**

- **No $\hbar$, no energy–time budget.** QFT's virtual pairs live on borrowed energy for $\Delta t \sim \hbar / \Delta E$. The framework has no $\hbar$ and no scale; it can say "short-lived / undifferentiated" but cannot derive the budget or any lifetime.
- **"Off-shell" is not captured.** A virtual particle's defining property is being off the mass shell ($E^2 - p^2c^2 \neq m^2c^4$) — a statement about *energy-momentum*, which the framework maps nothing onto. The framework's polar pair matches the *charge/quantum-number* sector, which is exactly conserved; it does not reach the energy-momentum sector where "virtualness" actually lives. So the framework's "unwitnessed" is a structural gloss on "not on-shell / not asymptotically observable," not the physical definition.
- **No propagator.** The negation orbit $A \to -A \to A$ is a pleasing image of appear-and-annihilate; it is not the Feynman propagator $1/(p^2 - m^2 + i\epsilon)$, and the overlay should not pretend otherwise.
- **Perturbative only.** Nonperturbative vacuum structure (condensates, instantons, the QCD vacuum) is untouched.

The honest summary: **the overlay is the framework's cleanest qualitative contact with physics — because it is structural rather than fitted — and it lands one genuine two-derivations-one-conclusion hit (the third-body requirement). It does not reach the quantitative heart of what makes a particle virtual (the mass shell and $\hbar$), and it takes an ontological side it should own explicitly.** Tier 1 is the case for taking it seriously; Tier 3 is the discipline that keeps it from overreaching.
