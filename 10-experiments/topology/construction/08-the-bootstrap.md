## What the bootstrap requires

A lens of the generator is **self-grounding** if, once unfolded, it contains structures that articulate the generator's own postulates and standing properties in the lens's vocabulary. This is the [self-bootstrap principle](../../../02-the-process/self-bootstrap.md) applied at the level of the topology experiment.

What the topology experiment has produced through Phases I, II, and III:

- The directed 3-cycle (Phase I) — the minimum stable witnessing structure.
- Chirality and the double triangle (Phase I) — both chiralities forced by Polarity at the cycle level.
- The 6 elements with anti-nodes (Phase II) — Polarity at the node level.
- The static prism (Phase II) — the spatial configuration.
- The dynamic twisted strand (Phase II) — the iteration trajectory; configuration ↔ process polar partnership.
- The bisection (Phase III) — algebraic assignment producing the four-structure $\{\phi, \psi, 1, -1\}$.

The bootstrap question: do these structures articulate the framework's seed?

## The bootstrap walk

### Postulates

**Conservation ($\Sigma = 0$).** Articulated at three levels:

1. **Polar matching level.** Each polar pair sums to zero: $\phi + (-\phi) = 0$, $\psi + (-\psi) = 0$, $1 + (-1) = 0$. The 3 polar matching relations encode additive Conservation directly.
2. **Cycle closure level.** Each chiral 3-cycle closes — traversing $A \to B \to C \to A$ returns to the start. The closure articulates conservative balance: no net displacement after a full traversal.
3. **Strand closure level.** The dynamic twisted strand $A \to -B \to C \to -A \to B \to -C \to A$ closes after visiting all 6 elements. The trajectory's closure is dynamic Conservation.

The framework's seed $\Sigma = 0$ is regenerated as **multi-level closure**: polar pairs sum, cycles close, strand traversal closes. Every level articulates Conservation in topology's vocabulary. ✓

**Existence ($\exists$).** Articulated at multiple levels:

- The 6 elements are non-trivial structural objects (each has determinate position, witnessing relations, polar partner).
- The two chiral cycles are non-trivial cycle structures.
- The four-structure $\{\phi, \psi, 1, -1\}$ contains four distinct algebraic values.

Non-trivial structure exists at every level of articulation. ✓

**Internality (no outside).** Articulated by:

- All structural relations (witnessing edges, polar matching, chirality) are internal to the topology lens.
- No external reference is invoked; the witnessing closes within each cycle, the polar matching is structural balance, the chirality is intrinsic to directedness.
- The bisection assigns algebraic values internally — values from the math experiment's value system, which is itself internally articulated.

The topology lens is self-contained. ✓

The polar structure pervades the topology experiment at every level. ✓

**Self-Reference.** Articulated at multiple levels:

All relations are internal. ✓

## Cross-lens convergence with math

The bisection in Topic 07 produces the four-structure $\{\phi, \psi, 1, -1\}$ within topology's 6 elements. The math experiment's algebra subdomain produces the same four-structure via Vieta on the self-reference equation $x^2 = x + 1$.

**Two genuinely independent commitment chains:**

- **Math path:** arithmetic (C1, C2, C3, C4, C5) + algebraic articulation (A1) → self-reference equation → roots $\phi, \psi$ → Vieta → four-structure.
- **Topology path:** topology substrate (T1) + connectivity (T2) → directed 3-cycle → chirality → double triangle → 6 elements (Polarity at node level) → bisection → four-structure.

**Shared commitments:** both paths use the framework's postulates and standing properties, plus the algebraic value system. They do not share lens-specific commitments (math's binary polarity choice C1; topology's substrate T1; etc.).

**Distinct commitments:** the lens choice itself, the substrate, the articulation language, and the specific within-lens commitments are all distinct between the two paths.

**The four-structure is the convergence point.** Both paths produce $\{\phi, \psi, 1, -1\}$ with the same polar relationships:

- Additive polar pair: $\{1, -1\}$ — articulated as Vieta sum + Vieta product (math) and as polar matching of the unit (topology).
- Multiplicative polar pair: $\{\phi, \psi\}$ — articulated as eigenvalues of the self-reference equation (math) and as the labeled eigenvalue positions in topology's chiral cycle (topology).

This is the framework's first substantive cross-domain finding under the [transferability principle](../../../15-cross-domain-analysis/transferability.md). Two non-shared commitment chains converging on the same forced atom is strong evidence that the four-structure is generator-level — a structural fact about the framework's seed, not an artifact of any single lens.

## What this demonstrates

The topology experiment, as a whole, articulates the framework's seed and converges with the math experiment on the four-structure. Specifically:

- **Internal coherence:** topology's structures (cycles, chiralities, polar matching, strand, bisection) are internally consistent and articulate the framework's seed without external scaffolding.
- **Cross-lens convergence:** the four-structure $\{\phi, \psi, 1, -1\}$ is produced by two independent paths, providing structural evidence that the framework's articulation is not lens-specific.
- **The topology experiment is bootstrap-verified.**

## What this does *not* demonstrate

The bootstrap demonstrates internal coherence and cross-lens convergence at the structural level. It does not:

- Prove the framework correct in any classical sense.
- Establish that topology articulates physical reality (that is the [physics experiment](../../physics/)'s domain).
- Establish that all lenses converge on the four-structure (more lenses must be tested).

## What this enables

The topology experiment, like the math experiment, is now **a bootstrap-verified atom** at the level of self-grounding lenses (see [a bootstrapped package is itself an atom](../../../02-the-process/atoms.md)).

Subsequent experiments (physics, biology, additional foundational lenses) can build on either or both of the math and topology packages. Where they share commitments with these packages, they inherit the four-structure structurally. Where they engage genuinely new substrates (continuous time, biological structure, etc.), they are subject to their own bootstrap verifications.

The cross-lens convergence between math and topology is the framework's first substantive empirical finding under the [strong hypothesis](../../../15-cross-domain-analysis/the-strong-hypothesis.md): the four-structure is a forced atom of the generator, not lens-specific. This finding can now be tracked in [observations.md](../../../15-cross-domain-analysis/observations.md) and used to predict what subsequent lenses (physics, biology) should articulate when they engage the four-structure.

The topology experiment is complete.
