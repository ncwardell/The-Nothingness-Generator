# 03. The Double Triangle

> **Polarity at the structural level forces the chiral 3-cycle to coexist with its anti-chiral twin. Both chiralities of the directed 3-cycle are present in the totality.**

---

## Question

[Topic 02](02-chirality.md) established that the directed 3-cycle has intrinsic chirality, with two distinguishable enantiomers $\alpha$ and $\beta$. By [Polarity](../../../03-the-grammar/02-polarity.md), every non-trivial structure has a compensating contribution somewhere. *What is the polar partner of the chiral 3-cycle, and what does its coexistence with the original articulate?*

## Dependencies

- [01. The Triangle](01-the-triangle.md) — directed 3-cycle as minimum stable witnessing topology.
- [02. Chirality](02-chirality.md) — directed 3-cycle has intrinsic handedness.
- [Polarity (weak form)](../../../03-the-grammar/02-polarity.md) — every non-trivial structure requires compensating contribution.

## Commitment

None. The double triangle is forced by Polarity applied at the chirality level.

## Construction

Topic 02 established two distinct chiralities of the directed 3-cycle on nodes $\{A, B, C\}$:

- $\alpha$: $A \to B \to C \to A$
- $\beta$: $A \to C \to B \to A$

These cycles are enantiomers — related by edge-reversal, not equivalent under any orientation-preserving operation within the directed-graph substrate.

By [Polarity (weak form)](../../../03-the-grammar/02-polarity.md), every non-trivial structure in the totality has a compensating contribution. Chirality $\alpha$ is non-trivial structure (a directed cycle with handedness). Its compensating contribution must exist somewhere in the totality.

The polar partner of $\alpha$ is exactly $\beta$:

- $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are constituted by opposite directedness — each is the negation of the other at the edge-direction level.
- No other structure available within the topology lens compensates for $\alpha$'s chirality except $\beta$'s opposite chirality.
- The negation operation at the chirality level (edge-reversal applied to each edge of the cycle) maps $\alpha \leftrightarrow \beta$.

Therefore: the chiral 3-cycle $\alpha$ and the anti-chiral 3-cycle $\beta$ coexist in the totality. **Both are present** — neither alone satisfies Polarity at the structural level.

This is the **double triangle**: two directed 3-cycles of opposite chirality, both present, structurally articulating the polar partnership of chirality.

## What is forced and what requires further commitment

**Forced by this topic:**
- Both $\alpha$ and $\beta$ exist in the totality.
- They are polar partners at the chirality level.
- Each, individually, satisfies the minimum-cycle requirement (Topic 01) and is internally non-complement-witnessing.

**Not yet articulated (deferred to Phase II):**
- The precise structural relation between $\alpha$ and $\beta$: disjoint (6 nodes total), sharing one node ("bridge," 5 nodes), or interleaved on the same 3 nodes with opposite-direction edges.
- The element count of the combined structure.
- The identification of any shared bridge element.

These require additional commitments about how the two cycles are embedded together. By [atomicity](../../../02-the-process/atoms.md), the minimum embedding is a candidate, but the specific embedding is not strictly forced by Polarity alone.

## All four standing properties (and Conservation) respected

- **Conservation ($\Sigma = 0$):** ✓ — the two chiralities sum to zero at the chirality-contribution level. The polar pair of cycles is balanced.
- **Polarity:** ✓ — directly forced by Polarity applied at the chirality level. The topic *is* Polarity at this level.
- **Closure:** ✓ — both cycles are internal; no external orientation is imposed.
- **Self-Reference:** ✓ — each cycle is self-referential (closes on itself); the double triangle is the polar-self-reference of the cycle structure.
- **Internality of Relations:** ✓ — the polar relation between $\alpha$ and $\beta$ (edge-reversal at the cycle level) is internal to the topology lens.

## Comparison

In knot theory, every chiral knot has its mirror-image partner. The trefoil knot exists as both left-handed and right-handed forms, related by mirror reflection. The framework's double triangle has the same structural form at the simpler 3-cycle level.

In Hegelian dialectics, the synthesis step often produces a doubled structure rather than a collapsed one — thesis and antithesis coexist within a higher articulation, not eliminated by it. The double triangle is structurally cognate.

## Transferability

**Engages:** Polarity + chirality + the directed cycle structure.

**Prediction:** Any lens with directed minimum-stable structures and Polarity articulates the coexistence of both chiralities.

- **Physics:** CP partnership in QFT — every chiral process has an anti-chiral one. CP violation indicates the partnership has internal structure (asymmetric coupling) but does not negate the existence of the polar partner; both chiralities are present in the framework.
- **Chemistry:** racemic mixtures contain both enantiomers; biological systems often select one preferentially, but the polar partner exists structurally.
- **Biology:** DNA's double helix is itself a doubled-chirality structure (two strands wound around a central axis with structural asymmetries).
- **Music:** ascending and descending forms of asymmetric scales (melodic minor, etc.) coexist as polar partners in the available harmonic vocabulary.
- **Logic:** for every directed inference rule there is a contrapositive; both forms are part of the system.
