# 01. Polar Bisection

> **The postulates force a polar pair: a non-zero contribution and its compensating complement.**

---

## Question

The framework's seed has been planted: postulates ($\Sigma = 0$, $\exists$, no outside) plus standing properties (Closure, Polarity, Self-Reference, Internality of Relations). *What is the first non-trivial structure the seed forces in the arithmetic lens?*

## Dependencies

- [Postulates](../../../../01-postulates/): Conservation ($\Sigma = 0$), Existence ($\exists$), Internality.
- [Polarity (weak form)](../../../../03-the-grammar/02-polarity.md): the standing property already requires this.

No prior topics in this experiment.

## Commitment

**Strong polarity.** The compensating contribution required by [Polarity (weak)](../../../../03-the-grammar/02-polarity.md) is *concentrated in a single complement element*. This is the only commitment in this topic. It selects single-paired compensation over distributed compensation, on minimality grounds.

## Construction

By [Existence](../../../../01-postulates/existence.md) (in the non-trivial reading: existence-as-observed; see the postulate file), the totality contains a non-zero contribution $E$.

By [Conservation](../../../../01-postulates/conservation.md), the rest of the totality has conservative sum $-E$.

Under strong polarity (above), the rest is a single element. Call it $-E$.

The totality contains the **polar pair** $\{E, -E\}$ with $\Sigma_{\{E, -E\}} = 0$.

## Comparison

In standard mathematics, polar pairs are universal: $+/-$, $1/-1$, conjugate roots, bra/ket, etc. The framework's polar bisection is the structural origin of all such pairs.

## Transferability

**Engages:** Conservation + Existence + strong polarity.

**Prediction:** Any lens engaging the same features articulates a polar pair as its first non-trivial structure.

## What this enables

The polar pair has structural properties that follow immediately: its sum is zero, and its two elements are related by negation. Those are [02. Zero](02-zero.md) and [03. The polar relation](03-the-polar-relation.md).
