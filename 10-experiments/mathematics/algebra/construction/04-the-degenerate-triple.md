# 04. The Degenerate Triple

> **$\phi\psi + (\phi + \psi) = 0$. The algebra produced by self-reference regenerates Conservation in its own vocabulary.**

---

## Question

Vieta's relations give $\phi + \psi = 1$ and $\phi\psi = -1$. *What happens when these two relations are combined?*

## Dependencies

- [03. Vieta's relations](03-vietas-relations.md) — $\phi + \psi = 1$, $\phi\psi = -1$.

## Commitment

None. The identity is forced by combining Vieta's relations.

## Construction

From topic 03:

$$\phi + \psi = 1, \quad \phi \cdot \psi = -1$$

Sum the two:

$$\phi\psi + (\phi + \psi) = -1 + 1 = 0$$

This is the **degenerate triple identity**. Equivalently:

$$i^2 + 1^2 = 0^2 \quad\text{where } i = \sqrt{\phi\psi} = \sqrt{-1}$$

(The $i$ aspect is developed in topic 05.)

## Why "degenerate triple"

The name comes from the Pythagorean-triple form. A Pythagorean triple satisfies $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$, equivalently $a^2 + b^2 - c^2 = 0$ — the algebraic shape of a right triangle's three sides.

The identity rewrites as:

$$i^2 + 1^2 = 0^2$$

A Pythagorean-shaped identity with the hypotenuse forced to zero. The triple $(i, 1, 0)$ is **degenerate**: the two legs ($i$ and $1$) cancel rather than compose into a third side.

The degeneracy is structural, not defective. A genuine Pythagorean triple articulates geometric composition (third side from two). The degenerate triple articulates *Conservation* — two contributions whose balance forces the third term to zero. $\Sigma = 0$ is the shape composition takes when held under conservative balance.

The "triple" names the three-term form ($\phi\psi$, $\phi+\psi$, $0$); the "degenerate" names the third term being forced to zero by the conservative balance of the first two.
