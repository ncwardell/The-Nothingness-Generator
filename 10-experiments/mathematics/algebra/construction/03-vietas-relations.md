# 03. Vieta's Relations

> **The two roots of the self-reference equation satisfy: $\phi + \psi = 1$ and $\phi \cdot \psi = -1$.**

---

## Question

The two roots $\phi$ and $\psi$ exist (topic 02). *What relations do they satisfy with each other?*

## Dependencies

- [02. The two roots](02-the-two-roots.md) — $\phi$ and $\psi$ exist as solutions of $x^2 - x - 1 = 0$.

## Commitment

None. Vieta's formulas are forced.

## Construction

For a monic quadratic $x^2 + bx + c = 0$ with roots $r_1, r_2$, Vieta's formulas state:

$$r_1 + r_2 = -b \qquad r_1 \cdot r_2 = c$$

Applied to $x^2 - x - 1 = 0$ (where $b = -1, c = -1$):

$$\phi + \psi = 1 \qquad \phi \cdot \psi = -1$$

Both are direct consequences of the equation. No additional commitment is invoked.

## What these relations articulate

- **Sum equals the unit of distinction.** The two eigenvalues of self-reference sum to the unit "1" — the same value introduced as the labeling commitment in arithmetic.
- **Product equals the negative unit.** The two eigenvalues multiply to "$-1$" — the polar complement of the unit.

The two roots, together, articulate the polar pair $\{1, -1\}$ from arithmetic — but now at a higher algebraic level. The labels committed in arithmetic reappear as forced consequences of the eigenvalue structure.

## All four standing properties respected

- **Conservation:** $\phi\psi + (\phi + \psi) = -1 + 1 = 0$ — the two relations sum to zero, regenerating the conservative form. (This is the degenerate triple, topic 04.)
- **Polarity:** the sum and product are themselves a polar pair: $1$ and $-1$.
- **Closure:** ✓ both relations are algebraic identities in the value system.
- **Self-Reference:** the relations articulate the structural relation between the two eigenvalues of self-reference.
- **Internality of Relations:** the relations are internal — defined by operations on the roots.

## Comparison

Vieta's formulas are standard for any polynomial. The framework's contribution: applied to the self-reference equation, they yield $\{1, -1\}$ — exactly the labeled polar pair from arithmetic. This coincidence is structural, not stipulated.

## Transferability

**Engages:** Self-Reference + Polarity.

**Prediction:** Any lens engaging the self-reference equation articulates the same two relations: the eigenvalue sum equals the unit, and the eigenvalue product equals its negation.

## What this enables

Combining the two Vieta relations yields a single algebraic identity: [04. The degenerate triple](04-the-degenerate-triple.md).
