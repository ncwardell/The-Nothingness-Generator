## What this module is

Algebra is a subdomain of the [mathematics experiment](../mathematics.md). It is the lens that articulates the relations among arithmetic structures using variables, equations, and abstract algebraic identities.

This file is the **module overview**. The atomic step-by-step articulation lives in [`construction/`](construction/).

## Why algebra is the bootstrap-bearing lens

The framework's methodology — set up a totality, posit $\Sigma = 0$, generate seed terms from postulates, derive consequences through forced moves — *is* the algebraic axiomatic method applied to ontology. The framework articulates being using algebraic structure: variables, operations, identities, equations.

Algebra is therefore not merely "one subdomain among others." It is the subdomain where the framework recognizes itself. The bootstrap — the verification that a lens articulates the framework's seed in its own vocabulary — happens in algebra because algebra speaks the framework's own articulation language.

Pure arithmetic provides the substrate: specific values, specific operations, integer generation. Arithmetic alone does not bootstrap, because arithmetic produces only specific computations, not abstract relations. Algebra builds on arithmetic and articulates the *structure of the relations* — and that is what the bootstrap requires.
