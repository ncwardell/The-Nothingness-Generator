# Postulate 1 — Conservation

> **$\Sigma = 0$.** The totality sums to zero.

---

## Statement

The sum of all that exists is zero. There is no net content of reality; the totality is fully balanced.

## Status

Postulate. Not derived. Not provable from anything more primitive. The strongest possible symmetry constraint a totality can satisfy.

## What it does

- Forbids the existence of unbalanced quantity.
- Together with [Existence](existence.md), forces compensating contribution somewhere in the totality whenever any non-zero contribution is present (the standing property of [Polarity](../03-the-grammar/02-polarity.md)).
- Makes "creation" structurally impossible as net addition. Anything that appears must appear with its compensating opposite. Creation is *bisection*, never *addition*.

## What it does *not* do alone

Conservation alone is satisfied by the empty set. $\Sigma = 0$ over no elements is trivially true. Without [Existence](existence.md), no structure is required to instantiate.

## On the algebra of the sum

A reasonable objection: "$\Sigma = 0$ over what algebra, with what notion of summation?"

The answer is structural, not evasive: **the algebra in which $\Sigma = 0$ is formally evaluable is itself something the framework generates, not something it presupposes.** Importing a pre-existing arithmetic to ground the postulate would be importing semantics from outside the totality, which [Internality](internality.md) forbids. The postulate has to be prior to the machinery that would let it be formally checked.

Read this way, $\Sigma = 0$ is not an equation in a fixed algebra. It is a **higher-order constraint on the space of possible internal articulations**: whatever algebra emerges from running the generator must close to zero. The arithmetic chain in [`10-experiments/mathematics/arithmetic/`](../10-experiments/mathematics/arithmetic/) is what cashes this out — building, from within, the first formal apparatus in which $\Sigma = 0$ becomes a specific equation. That arithmetic is one articulation; other lenses produce their own.

Structurally this is the same opening as Hegel's *Logic*: pure being and pure nothing are indistinguishable precisely because the conceptual apparatus that would distinguish them has not yet been generated. Demanding it up front demands that the framework presuppose what it must derive.

## Why this and not weaker

Possible alternatives:

- $\Sigma = c$ for some non-zero constant $c$ — but then $c$ must come from somewhere outside the totality, violating [Internality](internality.md). Or it is itself a content that must be balanced, returning us to $\Sigma = 0$ at one level up.
- $\Sigma$ undefined / no global sum — but then no constraint, and structure is unconstrained, returning us to "anything goes."

$\Sigma = 0$ is the unique minimal nontrivial choice: the strongest constraint compatible with anything existing at all.

## Why this and not stronger

Possible alternatives:

- All elements identically zero — equivalent to the empty set. Forbidden by [Existence](existence.md).
- All elements paired into mutual annihilations and *resolved* — equivalent to the empty set after collapse. The framework explicitly *holds* the unresolved pairing as polarity.

So $\Sigma = 0$ is the maximum constraint short of forbidding any structure at all.

## In dialectical reading

Conservation is the **negative pole** of the founding dialectic. It says: *nothing*. The totality is balanced to nothing; there is no net content. Held alone, it would dissolve all structure. Its productive function is to be the constraint against which Existence pushes — generating polarity, then witnessing, then everything.

In [Cusa's terms](../00-orientation/logical-foundation.md), Conservation is the *minimum* in the *coincidentia oppositorum* of minimum-and-maximum. It is the void as a structural principle, not as a non-being.
