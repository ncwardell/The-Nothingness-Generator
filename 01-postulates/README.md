# The Three Postulates

*The atoms of commitment. The minimal starting point.*

---

The framework rests on three postulates. They are the **atoms of commitment** at the foundational level — irreducible, mutually independent, and jointly necessary. (See [atoms at each level](../02-the-process/atoms.md) for what atomicity means here.) None can be derived; each must be accepted as a starting condition. Together, they generate the framework's strict grammar.

| | Postulate | Statement | Without it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Conservation](conservation.md) | $\Sigma = 0$ | Existence is unconstrained — no balance is required. |
| 2 | [Existence](existence.md) | $\exists$ | Conservation is satisfied by the empty set — no structure is forced. |
| 3 | [Internality](internality.md) | Distinctions are sustained internally; no outside reference exists. | Pairs persist by external reference; no self-reference is forced. |

Each postulate is necessary. None alone is sufficient. **The unfolding is generated by the productive tension among all three held simultaneously.**

## What the three together require

- Conservation alone permits the empty set. → Existence rules that out.
- Existence alone is unconstrained. → Conservation forces compensating contribution wherever non-zero contribution exists.
- Conservation + Existence permit external bookkeeping. → Internality forbids it, forcing internal observation and self-reference.

The three postulates are mutually irreducible and jointly generative. Removing any one collapses the framework to a different position (negative theology, classical realism, or pure void-mysticism — see [what-this-is.md](../00-orientation/what-this-is.md)).

## What you are accepting

By accepting these three, you are committed — under the framework's [paraconsistent dialectical logic](../00-orientation/logical-foundation.md) — to the four standing properties of the totality articulated in [the grammar](../03-the-grammar/):

1. [**Closure**](../03-the-grammar/01-closure.md) — the totality has no exterior.
2. [**Polarity (weak form)**](../03-the-grammar/02-polarity.md) — non-zero contribution requires compensating contribution somewhere in the totality.
3. [**Self-Reference**](../03-the-grammar/03-self-reference.md) — every internal observation references something internal; complete description includes itself.
4. [**Internality of Relations**](../03-the-grammar/04-internality-of-relations.md) — relations between elements of the totality are themselves internal to the totality.

That is the entirety of what you are committed to by accepting the postulates. Anything richer (specific algebraic forms, specific topologies, specific dynamics) requires explicit additional commitments at the level of an [experiment](../10-experiments/).

The clean separation between *what the postulates force* and *what experiments commit to beyond the postulates* is documented at [the boundary](../03-the-grammar/05-boundary.md).
